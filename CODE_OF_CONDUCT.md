# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Leqad welcoming and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that support an open, respectful, and collaborative community.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Focus discussion on the learner, the evidence, and the work.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and repair their impact.
- Protect private account, CAD, and security information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, sexualized language, or unwelcome attention
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or personal attacks
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may issue a correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban according to the impact and pattern of behavior.

Report community conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Report security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.
